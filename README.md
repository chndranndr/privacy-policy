# KITA Console Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

## Overview

KITA Console is a Japanese learning app for study drills, spaced repetition review, JLPT practice, grammar learning, and optional AI-assisted study tools. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

## Information We Collect

KITA Console is designed to keep core study activity on your device. The app may store learning progress, selected JLPT level, review history, settings, and similar app preferences locally so the study experience can continue between sessions.

If you choose to configure optional AI features, you may enter your own provider settings, such as an API key and model configuration. These settings are used only to enable the optional AI tools you choose to use.

## How Information Is Used

- To save study progress, review state, and app preferences.
- To provide spaced repetition scheduling and progress tracking.
- To run optional AI study features when you explicitly configure them.
- To support app functionality such as text-to-speech where available.

## Optional AI Features

Some features may allow you to send text, images, or study prompts to an AI provider that you configure. When you use those features, the information you submit may be processed by that third-party provider according to its own terms and privacy policy. KITA Console does not operate those third-party AI services.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. KITA Console does not share your local study progress with third parties for advertising purposes. Information may be sent to third-party services only when required for a feature you choose to use, such as an AI provider configured by you.

## Data Storage and Security

Study progress and settings are stored locally on your device. If you enter sensitive configuration values such as API keys, the app uses available device storage mechanisms intended for app data. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure, but the app is designed to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information.

## Children

KITA Console is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
